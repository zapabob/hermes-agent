# Downstream policy

Hermes Agent Windows Workstation Edition is an unofficial Windows-first
downstream distribution. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nous
Research. Original Hermes Agent is developed by Nous Research and licensed
under the MIT License.

Official stable and public Hermes contracts are the preferred integration
boundaries. `upstream/main` is integration input, not downstream product
authority. Downstream features are not deleted merely because upstream has an
overlapping capability; replacement requires parity evidence and overlapping
implementations are composed around the official contract.

Windows 11 native remains Tier 1 independently of upstream platform priorities.
Security and data-integrity fixes may be backported independently, and stronger
verified downstream protections are preserved when fixes overlap.

Contributing a generally useful change upstream and developing this downstream
product are separate tasks. Upstream attribution and history remain intact,
while fork-owned policy, Windows behavior, long-lived service coordination, and
product features are maintained under explicit downstream boundaries.
